# Cambridge curriculum planning

## Overview

Use this skill when generating or refining a Cambridge teaching plan from student weaknesses.

## Instructions

1. Confirm the curriculum band (Cambridge Primary, Lower Secondary, Checkpoint, IGCSE, AS Level, A Level).
2. Keep lesson objectives concrete and age-appropriate for that band.
3. Prefer exactly 10 short lessons; each lesson must address at least one listed weakness.
4. Cap objectives and activities at 3 items each; homework is one short sentence.
5. Do not invent grades or exam scores; work only from provided weaknesses and subject.
